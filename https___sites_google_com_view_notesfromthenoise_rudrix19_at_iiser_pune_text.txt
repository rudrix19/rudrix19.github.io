Starting Fresh I joined IISER Pune in 2024 as part of the BS-MS dual degree program. Coming here felt like stepping into a space where learning wasn’t a race; it was a process, and often a messy, beautiful one. The culture at IISER is different. There’s no forced competition and no one-size-fits-all syllabus. Instead, it’s about asking better questions, revisiting old ones, and learning how to think across disciplines. academics so far Semester 1 Introductory Biology Experimental Biology Principles of Organic Chemistry Evolution of Earth and Life Calculus I Introductory Mechanics Physics Lab Introduction to Computing Semester 2 Introduction to Biomolecules Principles of Physical Chemistry General Chemistry Practicals I The Solid Earth Science and Society Calculus II Linear Algebra Introductory Electricity &amp; Magnetism Semester 3 Ecology and Evolution Principles of Inorganic Chemistry Introduction to Probability Introductory Quantum Mechanics Introduction to Climate Science Introduction to HSS General Chemistry Practicals II Mathematical Methods for Physics Semester 4 Genetics Analytical Chemistry Molecular Spectroscopy Classical Mechanics Thermal &amp; Statistical Physics Physics Lab II Thermodynamics Data Analysis Semester 5 (ongoing) Electrodynamics I Quantum Mechanics I Optics Electronics I with Lab Methods of Experimental Physics Statistical Mechanics I beyond the classroom What makes IISER Pune special to me isn’t just the lectures or labs, it’s the energy of the student community. Looking Around and Ahead Being here has reaffirmed that I don’t want to just “cover the syllabus.” I want to explore, go off-road, and engage with science deeply. I’m gradually figuring out where I’d like to contribute, whether that’s in theoretical physics, interdisciplinary interfaces, or science communication. What I do know is this: IISER Pune has given me the space to build slowly, steadily, and sincerely. And I’m grateful for that. Here, it’s okay to not know something, so long as you care enough to find out.
