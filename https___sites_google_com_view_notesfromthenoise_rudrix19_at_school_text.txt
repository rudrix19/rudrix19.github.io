The Early Chapters I studied at DAV Model School, IIT Kharagpur (2012 - 2024), where I spent most of my formative years. It was in those classrooms, surrounded by peers and mentors, that I began to shape how I think, learn, and question. I was fortunate to do well academically. I became the Class 10 School Topper in the CBSE Board Exams with 98.6% (excluding skill subjects) and later the Science Stream Topper in Class 12 with 95.8%. But for me, numbers were never the main driver. What truly stayed with me were the questions that made me think long after the bell rang and the thrill of figuring out something I didn’t understand the day before. Learning, Not Just Preparing As I stepped into senior school, I chose to prepare for competitive exams, not because I was chasing a particular college, but because it felt like a structured way to go deeper into subjects I enjoyed. I appeared for: JEE Mains 2024 – scored 98.5 percentile JEE Advanced 2024 – secured AIR 7183 IISER Aptitude Test 2024 – AIR 532 While these scores mattered in the short term, they weren’t what defined the journey. I was never fixated on cracking “an IIT” or reaching a cut-off. I was learning because I genuinely wanted to understand things more deeply. The Almost and the Arrival I had hoped to get into IISc Bangalore, a place that, for me, symbolised curiosity-led science. I didn’t make it. And yes, it did leave a mark. But looking back, I genuinely feel grateful to be at IISER Pune. It is where I was meant to be, a place that values exploration, depth, and interdisciplinary thinking. And I’ve never felt like I’m missing out. Sometimes, the path you land on makes more sense in hindsight. And this one, of learning, not chasing , still feels like the right one. &quot; I didn’t prepare for an exam. I prepared to understand. The ranks came and went, but the questions stayed with me.
